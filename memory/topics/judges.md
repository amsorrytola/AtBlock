> Last updated: 2026-09-08  
> Status: active

## Official ETHOnline criteria (finalist + general)

Source: https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/info/details

1. **Technicality** — hard problem, sophisticated solution  
2. **Originality** — new idea or creative solve  
3. **Practicality** — complete, usable today  
4. **Usability** — obvious interaction  
5. **WOW** — lasting impression  

Async first round screens ~top 20% for live finalist rooms. **Partner prizes are judged separately and asynchronously** and are the majority of payouts. Winning a partner track does **not** require making finals.

Partner selection rule: **max 3 partner prizes**; all tracks under that partner are eligible.

## Demo / process rules that DQ more teams than ideas do

- Final submit: **Sun Sep 13, 2026 12:00pm EDT**
- Demo video **2–4 min**, **≥720p**, no phone cam, no AI voiceover, no music-as-narration, no speed-up
- Git history must show progress (no single final-day dump)
- Classic: work starts after official start; public starter kits OK
- AI is allowed as **assist**; attribute files/tools. User must still contribute (direction, check-ins, **human-narrated video**, feedback sessions). A 100% unmanned AI dump can be ruled ineligible.

## What recent Graph winners actually did (Lisbon 2026)

Sponsors paid for: reusable AI tooling, agents as data consumers, **standardized schemas**, **provenance** (pinned deployment IDs, freshness gates), and streaming when the agent spends.

Implication for us: a pretty dashboard loses. A **cite/pin/reject-if-stale** loop wins.
